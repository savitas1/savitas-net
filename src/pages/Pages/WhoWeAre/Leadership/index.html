Jamieson Gill

Jamieson Gill has worked with information systems for more than 25 years. He is a business and computer systems professional with experience across many industries.

Mr. Gill has extensive experience in small business operations in multiple industries and verticals. He believes the business information system is a critical part of a successful business, and he works with companies that want to use technology as a differentiator and a strategic advantage in their market.

Education

Mr. Gill holds a BS in Management Information Systems from the College of Business (now the Saunders College of Business) at Rochester Institute of Technology in Rochester, New York.

His studies and experience include accounting, production management, organizational behavior, general management, marketing, systems analysis and design, programming, computer and communications technology, finance, network applications development, project management, and leadership and team development, along with the fundamental organizational and technical principles of running a business.
