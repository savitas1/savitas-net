Constant Contact Partnership

Savitas partners with Constant Contact, the email marketing service, to help you stay in touch with customers, grow your customer base and sell more.

Constant Contact gives you easy-to-use tools and customizable templates for communicating with customers regularly and safely. Staying in touch leads to more repeat business and referrals.

Get started through Savitas

Sign up for Constant Contact through the Savitas partnership. Constant Contact sets its own plans, prices and trial terms; check its site for current details, and contact us to ask whether partner discounts are available.

Constant Contact can help you set up your first campaign and answer technical and marketing questions. Its Knowledge Base lists current support options.

Already a Constant Contact customer?

To move an existing account under the Savitas partnership, contact Constant Contact partner support (see the Knowledge Base for current contact details) and give them your account username and the partner name Savitaspa.
