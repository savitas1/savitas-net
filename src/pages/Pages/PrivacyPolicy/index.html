What this website collects

This is a static website. It has no user accounts, no sign-in and no customer portal, and it does not store anything you type.

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, social media plugins or tracking pixels.
  • No tracking cookies. This site does not set cookies to track or profile visitors.
  • Hosting logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Savitas does not receive these logs.

The appointment request form

The Request Appointment form does not send data to any server. When you submit it, it opens your own email app with a pre-filled message to sales@savitas.net containing the details you entered. Nothing reaches us unless you choose to send that email.

Email you send us

Email to Savitas addresses is handled by Microsoft 365. Microsoft processes it under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We use the information in your email, such as your name, email address, phone number and the details of your request, to reply to you and to provide the services you ask about.

Your choices

You can email us at privacypolicy@savitas.net or write to the address above to:

  • ask what information we hold about you;
  • have that information corrected;
  • ask us to stop contacting you.

Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, such as partner and vendor sites. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover.

Changes to this policy

If our information practices change, we will post the updated policy on this page and change the date above.

If you feel this site is not following this policy, please contact us at the addresses above.
