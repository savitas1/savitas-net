Advanced Solutions

Advanced solutions are a hybrid offering for projects working with multiple solution offerings, and/or some extremely new technology.

An example of an advanced solutions might be using a Raspberry Pi device that is configured automatically by the thousands to perform a specific task, and then have those devices connect to the cloud as Internet of Things (IoT) devices, reporting on data they are collecting, and possibly getting command and control data back from the cloud bus.
