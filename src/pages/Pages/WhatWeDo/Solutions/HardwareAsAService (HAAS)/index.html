Hardware as a Service (HaaS)

Savitas provides Hardware as a Service (HaaS) solutions.

HaaS works much like software licensing: instead of buying equipment outright, you pay for the hardware you use as part of a managed service.

That makes your hardware costs more predictable and keeps them in line with your growth.
