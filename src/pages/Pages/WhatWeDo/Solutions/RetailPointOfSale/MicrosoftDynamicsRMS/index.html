Microsoft Dynamics RMS

Microsoft Dynamics Retail Management System (RMS) reached the end of its support lifecycle on July 13, 2021. Savitas supports retailers who still run it and helps them plan a move off it.

What end of support means

RMS no longer receives fixes or security updates from Microsoft (see Microsoft's lifecycle page for Dynamics RMS 2.0). The software keeps running, but every year it gets harder to keep secure, to keep compliant with card payment rules, and to run on current versions of Windows and SQL Server.

How Savitas helps

Keep RMS running

We know RMS well, from store operations to multi-store setups and reporting, and can support your system while you decide what comes next.

Plan the move

We review your data, hardware, integrations and add-ons, and help you choose a replacement and a migration plan that keeps your stores trading.
