Retail Management Hero

Still on Microsoft Dynamics RMS, or looking to replace your current point of sale system? Retail Management Hero (RMH) is designed for exactly that move.

RMH was built as a replacement for Microsoft Dynamics RMS, which Microsoft stopped supporting on July 13, 2021. Because it works much like RMS, staff who know RMS adapt to it quickly, and the transition is easier than switching to an unrelated system.

Savitas helps you plan the move: what happens to your data, which of your existing hardware you can keep, and what to do with any add-ons or integrations tied to your current system.
