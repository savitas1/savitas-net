Retail Point of Sale Solutions

Savitas delivers and supports retail point of sale (POS) systems, from the first conversation to day-to-day support.

We have long experience with Microsoft Dynamics RMS, and we deliver POS solutions based on Retail Management Hero (RMH). Microsoft ended support for RMS on July 13, 2021, so if you still run it, we can keep it working while you plan your move.

What we do

  • Discuss your needs and concerns in detail
  • Quote everything you need to get up and running
  • Support you day to day so your POS system helps you succeed
  • Integrate your POS system with your other systems
  • Advise on, install and configure add-ons such as loyalty programs, e-commerce sites and advanced reporting
  • Make changes your systems need for compliance
  • Help you set up a merchant account to accept credit cards, Apple Pay and other electronic payments
  • For larger operations, work with your central office or distributor to streamline ordering, fulfillment and delivery

Savitas has also written add-on software for Microsoft Dynamics RMS, so if your business has a special requirement, we can build for it.
