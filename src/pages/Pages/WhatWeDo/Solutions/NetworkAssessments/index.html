Network Assessments

Savitas performs network assessments that include:

  • an inventory of what equipment is part of a network
  • what type of network it is, wired/wireless, protocols
  • What type of switching is being used on the network
  • What type of router is being used at the network gateway
  • Who the ISP is, and what the service being subscribed to is
  • A LAN performance/speed test
  • A WAN/ISP performance/speed test
  • A list of any vulnerabilities or exposure points on the network

if you are having trouble with your network, a network assessment would most likely identify some source of the problems you are experiencing, and then based on those findings Savitas can recommend solutions to address any problems identified during the network assessment
