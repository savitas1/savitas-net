CIO Services

Savitas CIO services give your business access to a Chief Information or Technology Officer without needing to hire one.

What is included

The service includes, but is not limited to:

  • A monthly meeting with your management or executive team.
  • The services needed to manage and maintain your systems.

Your leadership can focus on running the business, knowing its technology decisions and services are in experienced hands.

Our goal is cost-effective solutions with a clear return on investment. Any project we take on can include a business ROI analysis, so each dollar spent supports your strategic and operational goals.

Who it suits

CIO services work well for a company that wants to make sound technology decisions and use technology in new, cost-effective ways, but is not yet ready for a full-time technology executive.

When your business reaches the point where hiring a CIO or building an IT team makes sense, our staffing services can help with the candidate search and selection.
