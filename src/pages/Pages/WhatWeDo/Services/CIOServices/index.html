CIO Services

Savitas CIO services are designed to give your business access to a Chief Information/Technology Officer without needing to hire one.

This service includes but is not limited to a monthly meeting with the clients management/executives, and the services required to manage and maintain the clients system.

This allows management/executives to focus on their business knowing they have the best technology solutions and services as a result of working with Savitas.

This is a great service for a company that wants to make the best technology decisions, and use technology in an innovative and cost effective way.

Once your business has reached the point where it makes sense to hire a CIO or begin building your IT staff, Savitas can help with the staffing and candidate search and selection.

Our goal is to deliver cost effective solutions that have a clear return on investment. Each of our projects can include a business return on investment analysis. We want to make sure each dollar spent meets the companies strategic and operational goals, and is a cost effective investment.
