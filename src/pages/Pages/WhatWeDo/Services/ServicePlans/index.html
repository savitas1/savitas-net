Service Plans

Service plans are the best choice if your need for support is predictable. They give you the most predictable costs and consistent service levels.

Your computers are tools for getting work done, and they need to run reliably. A service plan keeps them that way, so you spend more of your time working and less of it on computer problems.

How plans work

  • Included services: every plan includes remote monitoring, updates, antivirus and email protection, monthly reports, remote support and help desk services. See system maintenance plans for the full list.
  • Service level agreement: your service contract specifies a guaranteed response time.
  • Work outside the plan: billed at a discount on our standard hourly rate.
  • Policies: business hours, minimums, cancellations and after-hours rates are set out in our service policies.
