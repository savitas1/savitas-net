Retail Point of Sale Solutions

Savitas is an expert in retail Point of Sale Solutions.

We deliver point of Sale Solutions based on the Microsoft Dynamics RMS and Retail Management Hero Point of Sale software

We are a total solution provider, we can

  • Discuss your needs in detail, and any concerns you might have
  • Provide you with a quote that covers everything you need to get up and running
  • Be there to support you on a daily basis to help you succeed with you Point of Sale system
  • Integrate your Point of Sale solution with other systems
  • Extend the functionality of your system by advising on add-ons to it, and assisting with the process of installing and configuring those add-ons such as loyalty programs, e-commerce sites, advanced reporting
  • Support any needed changes to your systems for compliance purposes
  • Work with your business to get a merchant account setup to accept credit cards, Apple Pay and other payment types that can be processed electronically
  • For larger operations we can work with the central office or distributor to get the order, fulfillment, delivery and delivery processing streamlined for maximum efficiency and accuracy

Savitas has also written add-on software for Microsoft Dynamics RMS, so if your business has a special need, we are more than qualified to meet your needs.
