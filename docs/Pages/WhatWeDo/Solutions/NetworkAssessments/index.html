Network Assessments

If your network is giving you trouble, a network assessment will usually find the source. Based on what we find, Savitas recommends solutions to fix it.

What a network assessment covers

  • An inventory of the equipment on your network
  • The type of network: wired, wireless and the protocols in use
  • The switching used on the network
  • The router at the network gateway
  • Your internet service provider and the service you subscribe to
  • A LAN performance and speed test
  • A WAN and internet performance and speed test
  • A list of vulnerabilities and exposure points on the network

Talk to us about a network assessment

Tell us what problems you are seeing and we will plan an assessment around them.
