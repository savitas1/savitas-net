Solutions

Savitas solutions address the strategic challenges businesses face with their information systems. We build and deliver each one by working closely with the client and understanding what they need.

We help each client choose the solutions that fit their needs, then deliver them on budget and on schedule. Where it adds value, we work with established technology partners, because everyone involved wants the customer to succeed.

Our solutions

Managed Services

Fully outsourced IT for a fixed monthly fee: monitoring, maintenance, planning and vendor management.

Cloud Solutions

Experience across the major cloud platforms, with Microsoft Azure as our platform of choice.

Security

Planning, maintaining and, when needed, remediating the security of your computing systems.

Network Assessments

An inventory and performance review of your network, with recommendations for any problems we find.

System Assessments

A review of your hardware and software: warranties, licensing, requirements and performance.

Systems Integration

Connecting systems, from simple exports to real-time integrations across multiple platforms.

Solution Architecture

Business analysis, project planning and clear communication that hold a complex solution together.

Advanced Solutions

Projects that combine several of our solutions or work with very new technology, such as IoT.

Application Lifecycle Management

Help managing applications from planning through retirement.

Business Intelligence

Bringing data from multiple sources onto one dashboard so you can make better-informed decisions.

Document Management

Paperless offices and document workflows for review, approval and routing.

SharePoint Solutions

Portals, document handling and workflow automation built on SharePoint.

Finance and Accounting

Selecting, setting up and supporting small and mid-size accounting and reporting systems.

Retail Point of Sale

Point of sale systems for retailers, including support for businesses moving off Microsoft Dynamics RMS.

VoIP and Cloud Phone Systems

Setting up new phone systems or reviewing the one you have for cost, performance and scale.

Software as a Service

Cloud software subscriptions such as Microsoft 365.

Hardware as a Service

Hardware on a predictable monthly cost that grows with your business.

Tell us what you need and we will recommend a solution and prepare a proposal.
