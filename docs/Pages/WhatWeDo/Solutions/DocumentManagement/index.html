Document Management

Savitas has turned multiple offices paperless, eliminating the space needed to store paper documents and making every document available to the right person at the right time.

We can help you go paperless and reach your documents from anywhere.

Document workflows

We also build workflow solutions that manage the documents moving into and out of your business processes, including:

  • Editing and revision
  • Review and approval
  • Production
  • Managing and routing incoming documents

For an example, see how we used SharePoint to manage work order documents on a large repair program.

Talk to us about document management

We will look at how your documents flow today and how to take the paper out of it.
